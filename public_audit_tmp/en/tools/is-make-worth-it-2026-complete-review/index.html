Is Make Worth It in 2026? A Complete Review for Businesses and AI Automation

Discover whether Make is worth it in 2026. We analyze its features, pricing, advantages, limitations, and the scenarios where this automation platform delivers the greatest value for businesses and professionals.
July 4, 2026 · 5 min | Translations:
  • Português
  • Português

As Artificial Intelligence becomes deeply integrated into business operations, automation is no longer just a way to save time. In 2026, connecting systems, eliminating repetitive tasks, and embedding AI into workflows have become strategic priorities for organizations seeking greater efficiency and competitiveness. Within this landscape, Make has emerged as one of the most comprehensive visual automation platforms available.

Its visual workflow builder allows companies to integrate hundreds of applications without advanced programming knowledge, making automation accessible to both small businesses and enterprise technology teams.

But does the platform truly justify the investment? In this review, we examine its key capabilities, strengths, limitations, and the business scenarios where Make delivers the greatest return.

What is Make and why has it become a leading automation platform?#

Make uses visual workflow automation to connect applications, streamline business processes, and integrate Artificial Intelligence services.

Make is a visual automation platform that enables businesses to connect multiple applications through graphical workflows known as scenarios. Instead of performing repetitive manual tasks, organizations can design automated processes that transfer information seamlessly across different systems.

This approach has made Make a popular solution for companies seeking to eliminate repetitive work, integrate software ecosystems, and accelerate digital transformation without developing custom integrations from scratch.

Visual automation with enterprise-level flexibility#

One of Make’s biggest advantages is the freedom to design highly customized workflows using an intuitive visual interface.

Beyond simple integrations, users can implement conditional logic, filters, variables, data transformations, and multiple execution paths within the same automation scenario.

Built for the AI era#

Another reason behind Make’s rapid adoption is its seamless integration with modern Artificial Intelligence services.

Organizations can connect platforms such as OpenAI, Anthropic, and other AI providers to automate document analysis, generate content, classify information, and power intelligent customer interactions.

Which features make Make stand out in 2026?#

Advanced workflow capabilities allow organizations to automate marketing, sales, operations, customer service, and AI-powered business processes.

Make has evolved far beyond simple application integrations. Today, it enables organizations to build sophisticated business workflows that combine APIs, cloud services, enterprise applications, databases, and Artificial Intelligence into a single automation ecosystem.

Hundreds of native integrations#

The platform supports hundreds of widely used business applications, including CRM systems, email platforms, cloud storage services, databases, financial software, collaboration tools, and productivity suites.

This extensive ecosystem significantly reduces development time while accelerating enterprise automation initiatives.

Scalable for growing organizations#

Another major advantage is its ability to scale alongside business growth.

As companies introduce new systems or expand operations, existing scenarios can be enhanced without redesigning the entire automation architecture.

When is Make a better choice than other automation platforms?#

Make stands out in projects that require advanced visual workflows, complex integrations, and growing adoption of Artificial Intelligence.

Make is best suited for organizations that need flexible automation, seamless integration between multiple business systems, and detailed control over every step of their workflows. Its visual interface allows teams to build sophisticated automation without requiring extensive software development expertise.

Although many automation platforms compete in this space, Make has positioned itself as one of the strongest options by combining usability, scalability, and enterprise-grade flexibility.

When Make delivers the highest return#

The platform is particularly valuable for organizations looking to:

  • automate sales operations;
  • integrate CRM, ERP, and marketing platforms;
  • connect Artificial Intelligence services;
  • eliminate repetitive operational tasks;
  • create custom workflows across multiple systems;
  • accelerate digital transformation initiatives.

For businesses that rely on numerous cloud applications, replacing manual work with automated workflows can save dozens of hours every month while reducing operational errors.

When another platform may be a better fit#

Not every organization requires the advanced flexibility that Make provides.

Companies with very simple automation needs may find lightweight solutions sufficient during the early stages of adoption. Likewise, highly technical teams that prefer complete infrastructure control may choose open-source platforms that can be self-hosted and extensively customized.

Regardless of the platform selected, the long-term trend is clear: AI-powered automation is becoming a fundamental layer of modern business operations as organizations increasingly connect data, applications, and intelligent decision-making.

Is Make worth it in 2026?#

The answer is yes for businesses and professionals that view automation as a strategic investment rather than simply a productivity tool. Make has evolved beyond connecting applications and now serves as a platform for building intelligent workflows that combine Artificial Intelligence, APIs, cloud services, and enterprise software.

Its balance of flexibility, ease of use, and scalability allows it to support everyone from startups and small businesses to large organizations managing complex digital ecosystems.

Key advantages#

Among the platform’s strongest benefits are:

  • intuitive visual workflow builder;
  • hundreds of native integrations;
  • excellent compatibility with Artificial Intelligence services;
  • extensive workflow customization;
  • enterprise scalability;
  • significant reduction of repetitive manual work.

These capabilities explain why Make is consistently recommended as one of the leading automation platforms for businesses embracing AI-driven operations.

Points to consider before subscribing#

Before investing in the platform, organizations should keep several factors in mind:

  • highly complex workflows require thoughtful planning;
  • new users may experience an initial learning curve;
  • usage should be monitored to optimize operational costs as automation scales.

For businesses committed to increasing efficiency without proportionally expanding their workforce, these considerations are generally outweighed by the productivity gains achieved after deployment.

As Artificial Intelligence continues to reshape enterprise software, automation platforms will evolve from connecting applications to orchestrating AI agents, real-time decision-making, and intelligent business processes. Within this rapidly changing landscape, Make remains well positioned as one of the most capable platforms for organizations seeking to build scalable, AI-powered automation strategies for the years ahead.

Perguntas Frequentes

What is Make?

Resposta rápida: Make is a visual automation platform that connects applications and builds automated workflows without requiring advanced programming skills.

Make enables users to integrate hundreds of applications through visual scenarios, automating repetitive tasks, synchronizing data, and incorporating artificial intelligence into business processes.

Is Make worth it for small businesses?

Resposta rápida: Yes, especially for companies looking to automate workflows and reduce repetitive manual tasks.

Small businesses can use Make to integrate CRMs, email platforms, spreadsheets, marketing tools, e-commerce platforms, and AI services, improving productivity while reducing operational costs.

What is the difference between Make and Zapier?

Resposta rápida: Make offers greater workflow flexibility, while Zapier focuses on simplicity and faster automation setup.

Although both are automation platforms, Make provides more advanced workflow customization with detailed logic and branching, whereas Zapier is generally preferred for simpler automation scenarios.

Does Make work with Artificial Intelligence?

Resposta rápida: Yes. Make integrates with leading AI services including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and many others.

Make provides connectors and API integrations that allow businesses to incorporate AI into automated workflows for document processing, content generation, intelligent classification, customer support, and AI-powered business operations.

Who should use Make?

Resposta rápida: Businesses, marketing teams, sales departments, operations professionals, developers, and organizations seeking process automation.

Make is suitable for organizations of all sizes that need to integrate multiple systems, eliminate repetitive work, improve operational efficiency, and scale automation initiatives.
