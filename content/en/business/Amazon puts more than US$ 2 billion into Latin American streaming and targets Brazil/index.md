*Prime Video is stepping up its bet on Latin America in a move that goes far beyond new shows. With more than US$ 2 billion planned through 2030, Amazon wants to expand local content, sports and distribution, putting Brazil at the center of an increasingly strategic battle for audiences.*

## Amazon makes Latin America a priority for Prime Video

### The investment announced

**Amazon** announced on August 20 that **Prime Video** will invest more than **US$ 2 billion** across Latin America between 2027 and 2030. The money will go toward original programming, locally produced or acquired content and live sports rights.

The plan covers **Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia and Chile**, five of the company's key markets in the region. Amazon also plans to more than double the number of local original productions compared with 2026.

### More than 25 productions in 2027

The first major sign of this strategy is already scheduled for 2027, when Prime Video expects to release more than **25 local titles**, including scripted series, films, reality shows and other formats.

The move is significant because it shows Amazon is trying to tailor its catalog to local preferences while still giving productions the potential to reach international audiences.

## Brazil gains weight in Amazon's strategy

![Amazon Prime Video expands local content and sports in Brazil](imagem-1.webp)

*Brazil is among the markets receiving investment in local production and sports rights.*

### Brazilian content as a strategic asset

**Brazil** is directly included in **Amazon's** regional plan, rather than being treated simply as a consumer market. The company intends to expand local production and continue developing partnerships with Brazilian professionals and production companies.

The strategy is also tied to the development of the local creative industry. Amazon cites training programs and initiatives aimed at developing audiovisual talent in Brazil as part of its long-term investment.

### Sports expand the platform's reach

Sports are another important component. **Prime Video** has expanded its NBA coverage in Brazil and Mexico to more than 200 games per season and has also increased its presence in Brazilian soccer.

According to Amazon, more than 20 million Latin American households had watched sports on Prime Video in 2026 at the time of the announcement, surpassing the total recorded throughout 2025.

## Amazon is not betting on shows alone

### The model is becoming broader

The move shows that **Prime Video** is being positioned as an entertainment distribution platform, rather than simply a catalog of Amazon's own productions.

In addition to content from Amazon MGM Studios, the platform provides access to third-party services, ad-supported free channels and movie rentals or purchases. This combination expands the platform's monetization opportunities within the same environment.

### A strategy that goes beyond streaming

The expansion also needs to be viewed within **Amazon's** broader strategy, which combines e-commerce, advertising, devices, digital infrastructure and subscription services.

Prime Video follows the same logic: adding more services to the customer relationship can make the subscription more valuable while creating additional revenue opportunities across Amazon's ecosystem.

## The investment puts more pressure on competitors

![The streaming battle intensifies across Latin America](imagem-2.webp)

*Higher investment levels are increasing competition for audiences, local content and sports rights.*

### Local content becomes a competitive battleground

For competitors, **Amazon's** expansion means greater competition for productions, talent, rights and consumer attention.

The advantage is not simply about the number of titles available. Local productions can help streaming platforms build stronger connections with audiences in each country, while sports can generate recurring consumption around high-profile events.

### Sports can become an anchor

The expansion of sports is particularly strategic because live events can create a different frequency of engagement from movies and series.

For **Prime Video**, combining sports, local productions and international content allows the platform to occupy more moments of the subscriber's entertainment routine. That could increase the service's importance within Amazon's broader entertainment ecosystem.

## The move also creates opportunities for Brazil's creative industry

### More demand for production

The investment could benefit production companies, technical professionals, actors, writers and other businesses involved in Brazil's audiovisual industry.

Amazon itself says it plans to expand talent development and training initiatives across the region. In Brazil, its Brasil no Set program has reached different regions and works with production and professional training companies.

### The impact could extend beyond Prime Video

The potential impact is not limited to the titles released on the platform. Greater investment in production could strengthen the local industry and encourage new partnerships between streaming platforms, production companies and creative professionals.

That effect will need to be tracked over the coming years, but the scale of Amazon's announcement makes the move significant for Brazil's audiovisual market.

## Amazon is building a more integrated platform

![Prime Video combines original content, sports and entertainment services](imagem-3.webp)

*Prime Video is expanding its role by combining original content, sports and third-party services.*

### Entertainment as part of the ecosystem

The decision to invest more than US$ 2 billion in the region becomes even more significant when viewed alongside **Amazon's** broader digital strategy.

The company already uses **Prime** to connect multiple services with consumers. Prime Video expands that ecosystem into entertainment, while other initiatives strengthen Amazon's presence in infrastructure and technology.

Notícia Tech has already covered another part of this strategy, showing how **Amazon** is expanding into digital infrastructure through satellite internet: **[Amazon launches satellite internet to rival Starlink and accelerate the race for global digital infrastructure](https://noticiatech.com.br/en/business/amazon-launches-satellite-internet-to-rival-starlink-and-accelerate-the-race-for-global-digital-infrastructure/)**.

### What to watch next

The key indicator will be Prime Video's ability to turn this investment into recurring audience engagement and stronger local relevance.

The growth of Brazilian productions, the performance of sports rights and the expansion of third-party services will be important signals for determining whether the strategy can increase Prime Video's position in the Latin American market.

The move also reinforces a characteristic of **Amazon**: the company does not need to compete in just one category. By connecting commerce, subscriptions, entertainment, advertising and infrastructure, it can use different businesses to strengthen its relationship with the same consumer.

That logic helps explain why Prime Video's investment deserves attention beyond the streaming industry itself. The competition is not simply about which company will produce the next hit series, but which platform can become the most relevant destination for multiple forms of digital consumption.

**Brazil**, because of its market size and the strength of its audiovisual and sports industries, will be one of the key markets for measuring the success of this strategy.
